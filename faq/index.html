Guide FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to common questions. The guide explains everything in more detail.

What is this site?

Lastfmlists is a free site where you can explore your Last.fm listening history through lists, charts and games.

How do I use this site?

Type your Last.fm username on the main page to load your listening history. Once it's loaded, the Explore tab shows ready-made lists, and the Lists tab lets you filter your history by artist, album, track, time and more. The guide explains every setting.

Does it work with Spotify, Apple Music, etc.?

Yes. The site uses your Last.fm data, so anything you scrobble to Last.fm is included, whatever app you listen in.

Do I need to register?

No, your Last.fm username is enough. You never give a password.

Is it free?

Lastfmlists is 100% free and ad-free, but donations are appreciated if you like the site.

Where is my data stored?

Your listening history stays in your browser, and it's only kept after you click Save Data. The site sends your username to Last.fm to download your scrobbles, and anonymous usage counts to GoatCounter. Those counts never include your username or what you listen to.

Do I lose everything when I refresh?

Yes, unless you save your data first. Your list settings stay in the address bar, so the same list comes back once your data has loaded again.

Do I have to download everything again every time?

No. If you saved your data, the next load only downloads the scrobbles you made since then.

Can I share a list?

To share the list itself, use Export Image or Export Grid. To share the settings, copy the address bar: anyone who opens the link gets the same list for their own history once their data has loaded.

Why does loading take so long?

Loading large libraries can take a few minutes. If it takes over 15 minutes and seems stuck, save your data and refresh the page. The next load continues where it stopped. Load All Details can take hours, but for almost everything, Load Details is enough.

What's the difference between Load Details and Load All Details?

Load Details downloads extra Last.fm info for the artists, albums and tracks you play most: track length, genre and country tags, and global listener and play counts. Load All Details does the same for everything you've ever scrobbled, which can take hours. You can stop either one with Save Data and continue later. Load Details is enough for almost everything. More in the guide.

Why are some filters greyed out?

While your listening history is loading, filters can't be applied yet. Some filters also rely on Last.fm metadata, like track length, tags and global play counts. These stay disabled until you click Load Details.

Can I look at a friend's stats?

Yes, as long as their Last.fm listening history is public.

How do I find my longest listening streak?

Set “Sort lists by” to Most plays in a row to see what you played the most times back to back. For the most days in a row, use Most days in a row. There are weekly and monthly versions too. All the sorts are explained in the guide.

How do I make an album collage (3x3, 5x5)?

Click Export Grid at the top to turn your current list into an album collage. You can set its size and a few other options. More in the guide.

How do I make a bar chart race GIF?

Set Display mode to Bar Chart Race, pick the dates and speed, then click Export Image. The race is saved as a GIF. More in the guide.

Can I compare two lists?

Yes. Turn on Comparison in the filter panel, and the left and right lists get their own filters. More in the guide.

Are there games?

Yes, three: Higher or Lower, Fill the List and Put Them In Order. They use the history you've already loaded, so they start instantly. More in the guide.

Is there a mobile app?

Not yet, an Android app is in the works. Until then, the site works in your phone's browser.

How do I report a bug or support the site?

Message me on Reddit (u/Alet404) or open an issue on GitHub. You can support the site on Ko-fi.
