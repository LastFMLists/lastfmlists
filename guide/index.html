Getting started

What happens after you type a username: what gets downloaded, the live preview while loading, why big libraries take a while, the progress in the tab title and "Notify me".

Reading a list

List type (tracks, albums, artists, scrobbles), list length, the numbers in parentheses ("Show unfiltered stats in parentheses"), and clicking a row to open its page.

Sorts

Group the sorts instead of listing all 23: plain counts; spread (different days, weeks, months); streaks; records in a time window (single day, week, month, X hours); firsts and speed (first to X, fastest to X); average listening time; time spent and share of global scrobbles. Then explain X once, since its meaning changes with the sort.

Filters

One short part per panel group (Artist, Album, Track, Time, session and day starters). Apply Filters versus Reset, and the chips above the list. Use h3 headings for the groups if it gets long.

Recipes

5 to 8 "How do I see…" examples you'd actually use, each with its exact settings. Ideas: top albums of one summer, songs mostly played after midnight, artists you binged and dropped, your longest daily streak, your first 100 scrobbles. Give each one an h3 and its own id (e.g. id="recipe-summer") so it can be linked, and later get a "try it" link.

Load Details and Load All Details

What each one downloads, roughly how long it takes, which filters and sorts it unlocks, and which one most people need.

Charts and the bar chart race

Chart orientation and scale, the race settings (start and end date, update frequency, speed), and GIF export.

Comparison mode

What it's for, the left and right editing switch, and one good use, such as this year against last year.

Equations

Mark this as advanced. The three commands (filter, sort, unique), how fields and operators combine, and two or three worked pipelines explained line by line. Put pipelines in <pre><code> blocks. The placeholder in the Equations box is a good first example.

Artist, album and track pages

What's on them, "Show all lists", and how clicking a ranking jumps to the highlighted row.

Exporting

PNG of a list, the album cover grid, and the GIF from a race.

Games

One or two lines each for Higher or Lower, Fill the List and Put Them In Order, plus where your records are kept.

Saving and privacy

What Save Data does, that everything stays in the browser, and what GoatCounter counts.
